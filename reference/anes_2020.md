# American National Election Studies (ANES) (2020) data

Based on the 2020 ANES data with derived variables and subset to people
who completed both pre and post-election interviews

## Usage

``` r
anes_2020
```

## Format

A data frame with 7453 rows and 65 variables:

- `V200001`:

  double 2020 Case ID

- `CaseID`:

  double 2020 Case ID

- `V200002`:

  double Mode of interview: pre-election interview

- `InterviewMode`:

  factor Mode of interview: pre-election interview

- `V200010b`:

  double Full sample post-election weight

- `Weight`:

  double Full sample post-election weight

- `V200010c`:

  double Full sample variance unit

- `VarUnit`:

  factor Full sample variance unit

- `V200010d`:

  double Full sample variance stratum

- `Stratum`:

  factor Full sample variance stratum

- `V201006`:

  double PRE: How interested in following campaigns

- `CampaignInterest`:

  factor PRE: How interested in following campaigns

- `V201023`:

  factor PRE: Confirmation voted (early) in November 3 Election (2020)

- `EarlyVote2020`:

  factor PRE: Confirmation voted (early) in November 3 Election (2020)

- `V201024`:

  double PRE: In what manner did R vote

- `V201025x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Registration and early vote status

- `V201028`:

  double PRE: DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT

- `V201029`:

  double PRE: For whom did R vote for President

- `V201101`:

  double PRE: Did R vote for President in 2016 (revised)

- `V201102`:

  double PRE: Did R vote for President in 2016

- `VotedPres2016`:

  factor PRE: Did R vote for President in 2016

- `V201103`:

  double PRE: Recall of last (2016) Presidential vote choice

- `VotedPres2016_selection`:

  factor PRE: Recall of last (2016) Presidential vote choice

- `V201228`:

  double PRE: Party ID: Does R think of self as Democrat, Republican, or
  Independent

- `V201229`:

  double PRE: Party Identification strong - Democrat Republican

- `V201230`:

  double PRE: No Party Identification - closer to Democratic Party or
  Republican Party

- `V201231x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Party ID

- `PartyID`:

  factor PRE: SUMMARY: Party ID

- `V201233`:

  double PRE: How often trust government in Washington to do what is
  right (revised)

- `TrustGovernment`:

  factor PRE: How often trust government in Washington to do what is
  right (revised)

- `V201237`:

  double PRE: How often can people be trusted

- `TrustPeople`:

  factor PRE: How often can people be trusted

- `V201507x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Respondent age

- `Age`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Respondent age

- `AgeGroup`:

  factor PRE: SUMMARY: Respondent age (categorized)

- `V201510`:

  double PRE: Highest level of Education

- `Education`:

  factor PRE: Highest level of Education

- `V201546`:

  double PRE: R: Are you Spanish, Hispanic, or Latino

- `V201547a`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Race of R: White (mention)

- `V201547b`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Race of R: Black or African-American (mention)

- `V201547c`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Race of R: Asian (mention)

- `V201547d`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Race of R: Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander
  (mention)

- `V201547e`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Race of R: Native American or Alaska Native
  (mention)

- `V201547z`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Race of R: other specify

- `V201549x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: R self-identified race/ethnicity

- `RaceEth`:

  factor PRE: SUMMARY: R self-identified race/ethnicity

- `V201600`:

  double PRE: What is your (R) sex? (revised)

- `Gender`:

  factor PRE: What is your (R) sex? (revised)

- `V201607`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Total income amount - revised

- `V201610`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Income amt missing - categories lt 20K

- `V201611`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Income amt missing - categories 20-40K

- `V201613`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Income amt missing - categories 40-70K

- `V201615`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Income amt missing - categories 70-100K

- `V201616`:

  double RESTRICTED: PRE: Income amt missing - categories 100+K

- `V201617x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Total (family) income

- `Income`:

  factor PRE: SUMMARY: Total (family) income (21 categories)

- `Income7`:

  factor PRE: SUMMARY: Total (family) income (7 categories)

- `V202051`:

  double POST: R registered to vote (post-election)

- `V202066`:

  double POST: Did R vote in November 2020 election

- `V202072`:

  double POST: Did R vote for President

- `VotedPres2020`:

  factor POST: Did R vote for President

- `V202073`:

  double POST: For whom did R vote for President

- `V202109x`:

  double PRE-POST: SUMMARY: Voter turnout in 2020

- `V202110x`:

  double PRE-POST: SUMMARY: 2020 Presidential vote

- `VotedPres2020_selection`:

  factor PRE-POST: SUMMARY: 2020 Presidential vote
