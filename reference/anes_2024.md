# American National Election Studies (ANES) (2024) data

Based on the 2024 ANES data with derived variables and subset to people
who completed both pre and post-election interviews

## Usage

``` r
anes_2024
```

## Format

A data frame with 4764 rows and 60 variables:

- `CaseID`:

  double 2024 Time Series Case ID

- `Weight`:

  double Post-election raked weight: Face-to-Face (FTF), Web, &
  2016-2024 Panel samples combined \[FINAL\]

- `Stratum`:

  double Stratum: Face-to-Face (FTF), Web, & 2016-2024 Panel samples
  combined \[FINAL\]

- `VarUnit`:

  double PSU: Face-to-Face (FTF), Web, & 2016-2024 Panel samples
  combined \[FINAL\]

- `InterviewMode_Pre`:

  Factor Mode of interview: pre-election interview

- `InterviewMode_Post`:

  Factor Mode of interview: post-election interview

- `CampaignInterest`:

  Factor PRE: How interested in following campaigns

- `VotedPres2020`:

  Factor PRE: Did R vote for President in 2020 \[revised\]

- `VotedPres2020_selection`:

  Factor PRE: Recall of last (2020) Presidential vote choice

- `PartyID`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: Party ID

- `TrustGovernment`:

  Factor PRE: How often trust government in Washington to do what is
  right \[revised\]

- `TrustPeople`:

  Factor PRE: How often can people be trusted

- `Age`:

  double Respondent age on Election Day

- `RaceEth`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: R self-identified race/ethnicity

- `Sex`:

  Factor PRE: What is R's sex? \[revised\]

- `Income`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: Total (household) income

- `EarlyVote`:

  Factor PRE: Already voted in General Election

- `EarlyVoteGeneral`:

  Factor PRE: Confirmation voted (early) in November 5 election

- `Post_Vote24`:

  Factor POST: Did R vote in the 2024 election

- `Pre_VotePres24`:

  Factor PRE: Did R vote for President

- `Post_VotePres24`:

  Factor POST: Did R vote for President

- `VotedPres2024_selection`:

  Factor PRE-POST: SUMMARY: 2024 PRESIDENTIAL VOTE

- `AgeGroup`:

  Factor Respondent age on Election Day (categorized)

- `EducationGroup`:

  Factor Highest level of education (categorized)

- `Income7`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: Total (household) income

- `EarlyVote2024`:

  character PRE: DID R ALREADY VOTE

- `Voted2024`:

  character Voted in 2024 election

- `VotedPres2024`:

  character Voted for president in 2024 election

- `V240001`:

  double 2024 Time Series Case ID

- `V240002a`:

  double Mode of interview: pre-election interview

- `V240002b`:

  double Mode of interview: post-election interview

- `V240108b`:

  double Post-election raked weight: Face-to-Face (FTF), Web, &
  2016-2024 Panel samples combined \[FINAL\]

- `V240108d`:

  double Stratum: Face-to-Face (FTF), Web, & 2016-2024 Panel samples
  combined \[FINAL\]

- `V240108c`:

  double PSU: Face-to-Face (FTF), Web, & 2016-2024 Panel samples
  combined \[FINAL\]

- `V241005`:

  double PRE: How interested in following campaigns

- `V241103`:

  double PRE: Did R vote for President in 2020 \[revised\]

- `V241104`:

  double PRE: Recall of last (2020) Presidential vote choice

- `V241038`:

  double PRE: Did R vote for President

- `V241221`:

  double PRE: Party ID: Does R think of self as Democrat, Republican, or
  Independent

- `V241222`:

  double PRE: Party Identification strong - Democrat Republican

- `V241223`:

  double PRE: No Party Identification - closer to Democratic Party or
  Republican Party

- `V241227x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Party ID

- `V241229`:

  double PRE: How often trust government in Washington to do what is
  right \[revised\]

- `V241234`:

  double PRE: How often can people be trusted

- `V241458x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Respondent age on Election Day

- `V241463`:

  double PRE: Highest level of education

- `V241499`:

  double PRE: R: Are you Spanish, Hispanic, or Latino

- `V241501x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: R self-identified race/ethnicity

- `V241550`:

  double PRE: What is R's sex? \[revised\]

- `V241566x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: Total (household) income

- `V242065`:

  double POST: Did R vote in the 2024 election

- `V241035`:

  double PRE: Already voted in General Election

- `V241036`:

  double PRE: Confirmation voted (early) in November 5 election

- `V241037`:

  double PRE: In what manner did R vote

- `V242051`:

  double POST: Is R registered to vote

- `V242095x`:

  double PRE-POST: SUMMARY: VOTER TURNOUT IN 2024

- `V242066`:

  double POST: Did R vote for President

- `V241039`:

  double PRE: For whom did R vote for President

- `V242067`:

  double POST: For whom did R vote for President
