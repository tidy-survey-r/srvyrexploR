# American National Election Studies (ANES) (2024) data

Based on the 2024 ANES data with derived variables and subset to people
who completed both pre and post-election interviews

## Usage

``` r
anes_2024
```

## Format

A data frame with 4964 rows and 54 variables:

- `V240001`:

  double 2024 Case ID

- `CaseID`:

  double CASEID

- `InterviewMode_Pre`:

  Factor MODE OF INTERVIEW: PRE-ELECTION INTERVIEW

- `InterviewMode_Post`:

  Factor MODE OF INTERVIEW: POST-ELECTION INTERVIEW

- `Weight`:

  double FULL SAMPLE POST-ELECTION WEIGHT

- `Stratum`:

  double FULL SAMPLE VARIANCE STRATUM

- `VarUnit`:

  double FULL SAMPLE VARIANCE UNIT

- `CampaignInterest`:

  Factor PRE: HOW INTERESTED IN FOLLOWING CAMPAIGNS

- `VotedPres2020`:

  Factor PRE: DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT IN 2020 (REVISED)

- `VotedPres2020_selection`:

  Factor PRE: RECALL OF LAST (2020) PRESIDENTIAL VOTE CHOICE

- `PartyID`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: PARTY ID

- `TrustGovernment`:

  Factor PRE: HOW OFTEN TRUST GOVERNMENT IN WASHINGTON TO DO WHAT IS
  RIGHT (REVISED)

- `TrustPeople`:

  Factor PRE: HOW OFTEN CAN PEOPLE BE TRUSTED

- `Age`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: RESPONDENT AGE

- `Education`:

  double PRE: HIGHEST LEVEL OF EDUCATION

- `RaceEth`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: R SELF-IDENTIFIED RACE/ETHNICITY

- `Sex`:

  Factor PRE: WHAT IS YOUR (R) SEX? (REVISED)

- `Income`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: TOTAL (FAMILY) INCOME

- `EarlyVote2024`:

  Factor PRE: DID R ALREADY VOTE

- `VotedPres2024`:

  Factor POST: DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT

- `VotedPres2024_selection`:

  Factor PRE-POST: SUMMARY: 2024 PRESIDENTIAL VOTE

- `AgeGroup`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: RESPONDENT AGE (categorized)

- `EducationGroup`:

  Factor PRE: HIGHEST LEVEL OF EDUCATION (categorized)

- `Income7`:

  Factor PRE: SUMMARY: TOTAL (FAMILY) INCOME (7 categories)

- `V240001`:

  double CASEID

- `V240002a`:

  double MODE OF INTERVIEW: PRE-ELECTION INTERVIEW

- `V240002b`:

  double MODE OF INTERVIEW: POST-ELECTION INTERVIEW

- `V240108b`:

  double FULL SAMPLE POST-ELECTION WEIGHT

- `V240108d`:

  double FULL SAMPLE VARIANCE STRATUM

- `V240108c`:

  double FULL SAMPLE VARIANCE UNIT

- `V241005`:

  double PRE: HOW INTERESTED IN FOLLOWING CAMPAIGNS

- `V241103`:

  double PRE: DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT IN 2020 (REVISED)

- `V241104`:

  double PRE: RECALL OF LAST (2020) PRESIDENTIAL VOTE CHOICE

- `V241038`:

  double PRE: DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT

- `V241221`:

  double PRE: PARTY ID

- `V241222`:

  double PRE: PARTY ID STRONG

- `V241223`:

  double PRE: PARTY ID LEAN

- `V241227x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: PARTY ID

- `V241229`:

  double PRE: HOW OFTEN TRUST GOVERNMENT IN WASHINGTON TO DO WHAT IS
  RIGHT (REVISED)

- `V241234`:

  double PRE: HOW OFTEN CAN PEOPLE BE TRUSTED

- `V241458x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: RESPONDENT AGE

- `V241463`:

  double PRE: HIGHEST LEVEL OF EDUCATION

- `V241499`:

  double PRE: HISPANIC ETHNICITY

- `V241501x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: R SELF-IDENTIFIED RACE/ETHNICITY

- `V241550`:

  double PRE: WHAT IS YOUR (R) SEX? (REVISED)

- `V241566x`:

  double PRE: SUMMARY: TOTAL (FAMILY) INCOME

- `V242065`:

  double POST: DID R VOTE IN NOVEMBER 2024 ELECTION

- `V241035`:

  double PRE: DID R ALREADY VOTE

- `V241037`:

  double PRE: HOW VOTE

- `V242051`:

  double POST: REGISTERED TO VOTE

- `V242095x`:

  double PRE-POST: SUMMARY: VOTER TURNOUT IN 2020

- `V242066`:

  double POST: DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT

- `V241039`:

  double PRE: FOR WHOM DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT (2024)

- `V242067`:

  double POST: FOR WHOM DID R VOTE FOR PRESIDENT (2024)

- `V242096x`:

  double PRE-POST: SUMMARY: 2024 PRESIDENTIAL VOTE
