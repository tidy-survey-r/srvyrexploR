# Residential Energy Consumption Survey (RECS) (2020) data

Based on the 2020 RECS microdata with derived variables

## Usage

``` r
recs_2020
```

## Format

A data frame with 18496 rows and 118 variables:

- `DOEID`:

  double Unique identifier for each respondent

- `ClimateRegion_BA`:

  factor Building America Climate Zone

- `Urbanicity`:

  factor 2010 Census Urban Type Code

- `Region`:

  factor Census Region

- `REGIONC`:

  character Census Region

- `Division`:

  factor Census Division, Mountain Division is divided into North and
  South for RECS purposes

- `STATE_FIPS`:

  character State Federal Information Processing System Code

- `state_postal`:

  factor State Postal Code

- `state_name`:

  factor State Name

- `HDD65`:

  double Heating degree days in 2020, base temperature 65F; Derived from
  the weighted temperatures of nearby weather stations

- `CDD65`:

  double Cooling degree days in 2020, base temperature 65F; Derived from
  the weighted temperatures of nearby weather stations

- `HDD30YR`:

  double Heating degree days, 30-year average 1981-2010, base
  temperature 65F; Taken from nearest weather station, inoculated with
  random errors

- `CDD30YR`:

  double Cooling degree days, 30-year average 1981-2010, base
  temperature 65F; Taken from nearest weather station, inoculated with
  random errors

- `HousingUnitType`:

  factor Type of housing unit

- `YearMade`:

  factor Range when housing unit was built

- `TOTSQFT_EN`:

  double Total energy-consuming area (square footage) of the housing
  unit. Includes all main living areas; all basements; heated, cooled,
  or finished attics; and heating or cooled garages. For single-family
  housing units this is derived using the respondent-reported square
  footage (SQFTEST) and adjusted using the "include" variables (e.g.,
  SQFTINCB), where applicable. For apartments and mobile homes this is
  the respondent-reported square footage. A derived variable rounded to
  the nearest 10

- `TOTHSQFT`:

  double Square footage of the housing unit that is heated by space
  heating equipment. A derived variable rounded to the nearest 10

- `TOTCSQFT`:

  double Square footage of the housing unit that is cooled by
  air-conditioning equipment or evaporative cooler, a derived variable
  rounded to the nearest 10

- `SpaceHeatingUsed`:

  logical Space heating equipment used

- `ACUsed`:

  logical Air conditioning equipment used

- `HeatingBehavior`:

  factor Winter temperature control method

- `WinterTempDay`:

  double Winter thermostat setting or temperature in home when someone
  is home during the day

- `WinterTempAway`:

  double Winter thermostat setting or temperature in home when no one is
  home during the day

- `WinterTempNight`:

  double Winter thermostat setting or temperature in home at night

- `ACBehavior`:

  factor Summer temperature control method

- `SummerTempDay`:

  double Summer thermostat setting or temperature in home when someone
  is home during the day

- `SummerTempAway`:

  double Summer thermostat setting or temperature in home when no one is
  home during the day

- `SummerTempNight`:

  double Summer thermostat setting or temperature in home at night

- `NWEIGHT`:

  double Final Analysis Weight

- `NWEIGHT1 - NWEIGHT60`:

  double Final Analysis Weight for replicate 1-60

- `BTUEL`:

  double Total electricity use, in thousand Btu, 2020, including
  self-generation of solar power

- `DOLLAREL`:

  double Total electricity cost, in dollars, 2020

- `BTUNG`:

  double Total natural gas use, in thousand Btu, 2020

- `DOLLARNG`:

  double Total natural gas cost, in dollars, 2020

- `BTULP`:

  double Total propane use, in thousand Btu, 2020

- `DOLLARLP`:

  double Total propane cost, in dollars, 2020

- `BTUFO`:

  double Total fuel oil/kerosene use, in thousand Btu, 2020

- `DOLLARFO`:

  double Total fuel oil/kerosene cost, in dollars, 2020

- `BTUWOOD`:

  double Total wood use, in thousand Btu, 2020

- `TOTALBTU`:

  double Total usage including electricity, natural gas, propane, and
  fuel oil, in thousand Btu, 2020

- `TOTALDOL`:

  double Total cost including electricity, natural gas, propane, and
  fuel oil, in dollars, 2020
