# Residential Energy Consumption Survey (RECS) (2015) data

Based on the 2015 RECS microdata with derived variables

## Usage

``` r
recs_2015
```

## Format

A data frame with 5686 rows and 141 variables:

- `DOEID`:

  double Unique identifier for each respondent

- `REGIONC`:

  double Census Region (numeric)

- `Region`:

  factor Census Region (factor)

- `Division`:

  factor Census Division

- `MSAStatus`:

  factor Housing unit in Census Metropolitan Statistical Area or
  Micropolitan Statistical Area

- `Urbanicity`:

  factor Census 2010 Urban Type

- `HousingUnitType`:

  factor Type of housing unit

- `YearMade`:

  factor Year range when housing unit was built

- `SpaceHeatingUsed`:

  logical Space heating used

- `HeatingBehavior`:

  factor Main heating equipment household behavior

- `WinterTempDay`:

  double Winter temperature when someone is home during the day

- `WinterTempAway`:

  double Winter temperature when no one is home during the day

- `WinterTempNight`:

  double Winter temperature at night

- `ACUsed`:

  logical Air conditioning equipment used

- `ACBehavior`:

  factor Central air conditioner household behavior

- `SummerTempDay`:

  double Summer temperature when someone is home during the day

- `SummerTempAway`:

  double Summer temperature when no one is home during the day

- `SummerTempNight`:

  double Summer temperature at night

- `TOTCSQFT`:

  double Total cooled square footage

- `TOTHSQFT`:

  double Total heated square footage

- `TOTSQFT_EN`:

  double Total square footage (used for publication)

- `TOTUCSQFT`:

  double Total uncooled square footage

- `TOTUSQFT`:

  double Total unheated square footage

- `NWEIGHT`:

  double Final sample weight

- `BRRWT1 - BRRWT96`:

  double Replicate weight 1 through 96

- `CDD30YR`:

  double Cooling degree days, 30-year average 1981-2010, base
  temperature 65F

- `CDD65`:

  double Cooling degree days in 2015, base temperature 65F

- `CDD80`:

  double Cooling degree days in 2015, base temperature 80F (used for
  garage cooling load estimation only)

- `ClimateRegion_BA`:

  factor Building America Climate Zone

- `ClimateRegion_IECC`:

  factor IECC Climate Code

- `HDD30YR`:

  double Heating degree days, 30-year average 1981-2010, base
  temperature 65F

- `HDD65`:

  double Heating degree days in 2015, base temperature 65F

- `HDD50`:

  double Heating degree days in 2015, base temperature 50F (used for
  garage heating load estimation only)

- `GNDHDD65`:

  double Heating degree days of ground temperature in 2015, base
  temperature 65F

- `BTUEL`:

  double Total site electricity usage, in thousand Btu, 2015

- `DOLLAREL`:

  double Total electricity cost, in dollars, 2015

- `BTUNG`:

  double Total natural gas usage, in thousand Btu, 2015

- `DOLLARNG`:

  double Total natural gas cost, in dollars, 2015

- `BTULP`:

  double Total propane usage, in thousand Btu, 2015

- `DOLLARLP`:

  double Total cost of propane, in dollars, 2015

- `BTUFO`:

  double Total fuel oil/kerosene usage, in thousand Btu, 2015

- `DOLLARFO`:

  double Total cost of fuel oil/kerosene, in dollars, 2015

- `TOTALBTU`:

  double Total usage, in thousand Btu, 2015

- `TOTALDOL`:

  double Total cost, in dollars, 2015

- `BTUWOOD`:

  double Total cordwood usage, in thousand Btu, 2015 (Wood consumption
  is not included in TOTALBTU or TOTALDOL)

- `BTUPELLET`:

  double Total wood pellet usage, in thousand Btu, 2015 (Wood
  consumption is not included in TOTALBTU or TOTALDOL)
