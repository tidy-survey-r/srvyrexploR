# California Health Interview Survey (CHIS) (2023) data

A subset of variables from the CHIS 2023 Public Use File

## Usage

``` r
chis_2023
```

## Format

A data frame with 21671 rows and 98 variables:

- `PUF1Y_ID`:

  character PUBLIC USE FILE ID - CHIS 1 YEAR DATAFILES

- `AH1V2`:

  factor HAVE USUAL SOURCE OF HEALTH CARE

- `AH22`:

  factor DELAY/NOT GET OTHER MEDICAL CARE IN PAST 12 MOS

- `SMKCUR30`:

  factor CURRENT SMOKER (PAST 30 DAYS)

- `AB1`:

  factor GENERAL HEALTH CONDITION

- `DIABETES`:

  factor DOCTOR EVER TOLD HAVE DIABETES (NON-GESTATIONAL)

- `BMI_P`:

  double BODY MASS INDEX (PUF RECODE)

- `RBMI`:

  factor BMI DESCRIPTIVE

- `AB17`:

  factor DOCTOR EVER TOLD HAVE ASTHMA

- `DSTRS12`:

  factor LIKELY HAS HAD PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTRESS IN THE LAST YEAR

- `AB29V2`:

  factor DOCTOR EVER TOLD HAVE HIGH BLOOD PRESSURE

- `SPK_ENG`:

  factor ENGLISH USE AND PROFICIENCY

- `POVLL2_P1V2`:

  double POVERTY LEVEL AS TIMES OF 100% FPL (PUF RECODE V2)

- `POVLL`:

  factor POVERTY LEVEL

- `SRAGE_P1`:

  ordered;factor SELF-REPORTED AGE (PUF 1 YR RECODE)

- `SRSEX`:

  factor SELF-REPORTED GENDER

- `OMBSRR_P1`:

  factor OMB/CURRENT DOF RACE - ETHNICITY (PUF 1 YR RECODE)

- `RAKEDW0`:

  double CHIS 2023 FINAL RAKED WEIGHT

- `RAKEDW1 - RAKEDW80`:

  CHIS2023 RAKED WEIGHT - REPLICATE 1 through REPLICATE 80
